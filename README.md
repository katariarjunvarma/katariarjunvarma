## 🧠 About Me

Hey! I'm **Katari Arjun Varma**, an AI & ML engineering student from **Bhimavaram, Andhra Pradesh** 🇮🇳 — currently pursuing my **B.Tech in Computer Science (AI & ML)** at **Lovely Professional University, Punjab** (CGPA: **8.03/10**).

I scored a perfect **10.0 CGPA** in 10th and **95.4%** in my Intermediate (MPC) before moving into engineering, where I found what I actually enjoy: **building systems that run in production, not just demos.**

Since joining LPU in 2023, I've been shipping beyond the classroom:

- 🏫 Built **CampusOne** — a live, full-stack Django campus platform with **OpenCV LBPH face-recognition attendance**, role-based access for 4 user types, and a PostgreSQL production database on Render
- 🎯 Built an **Elective Recommendation System** (React + TypeScript) suggesting from **100+ courses** with seat-tracking and admin approval workflows
- 📜 Earned the **Oracle OCI AI Foundations** and **Mendix Rapid Developer** certifications

I work across the full stack — **Python, Django, FastAPI, React, TypeScript** — and on the ML side with **TensorFlow, Scikit-learn, and OpenCV**. I care about systems that are reliable, deployed, and understood end to end.
